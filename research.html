Visual Completion Phenomena

Do perceptual completion effects found under controlled laboratory conditions still hold in real-world settings? In Prof. Marianne Maertens's group, which studies visual perception through psychophysical experiments and computational modeling, I assist on two experiments on visual completion, specifically amodal completion of occluded surfaces. I collect behavioral data in real-world experimental setups and contribute to data processing and analysis in Python and R, using Git for version control.

December 2024 Poster · 5th National Social Psychology Conference, Ankara

Threatened Masculinity: An Examination of Sexist and Objectifying Attitudes

Does a threat to men's sense of masculinity make them more likely to hold sexist and objectifying attitudes toward women? I proposed this sub-study within Dr. Ece Akça-Doğan's TÜBİTAK 1002-A project, designed the masculinity-threat manipulation, collected data from 96 participants and ran the analyses.

Although the manipulation check suggested the threat had worked, we found no evidence that it increased sexist or objectifying attitudes.

Disability, Reproduction & Narrative

2022 – 23 Research Assistant, STAR Scholarship
Ege Evolutionary Psychology Lab

The Behavioral Immune System

What are the social consequences of the behavioral immune system, the reactions that steer us away from possible sources of disease, and do genes moderate it? For this TÜBİTAK project, I collected behavioral data from 100+ participants, and coordinated with a molecular microbiology team handling saliva samples for DNA analysis.

Seeing behavioral data paired with genetic data was what first made me curious about measuring the mind beyond what people do and say.
