Outside the lab

Outside the lab, much of my work has been with people: women and children affected by violence, high-school students, children with fewer educational opportunities, and colleagues from many cultures. Looking back, it circles the same themes as my early research: gender, violence, and who gets access to what.

Violence prevention & gender

Intern Psychologist

Izmit Violence Prevention and Monitoring Center, Türkiye · 2023

Through the Presidential National Internship Program, I supported women and children affected by psychological, sexual and physical violence. I conducted face-to-face interviews with more than 10 victims in forensic cases and wrote detailed reports, and followed up by phone with more than 150 victims to keep records and reporting accurate.

Safe Relationships Project

Ege University & Ministry of Education, Türkiye · 2021–22

I led a project to help high school students recognize when certain behaviors in their current or future romantic relationships amount to psychological violence. Through a series of seminars, we built students' awareness and understanding of what psychological violence looks like.

Menstrual Awareness Project

Fontys University, Netherlands · 2023

With two fellow exchange students, I ran workshops to raise men's awareness of menstruation, and we reached an agreement with the university to stock hygienic menstrual products in every campus restroom.

See Me, Tell Me Project

Community Volunteers Foundation, Türkiye · 2021–22

Through this project, I contributed to the social, cultural and economic development of women in prison. I collected donations of money and books for the initiative and kept accurate records of every contribution, so they could be distributed transparently and properly.

Education & intercultural work

Fernweh Consulting

Founder & Consultant · 2025–now

Having gone through Erasmus, a traineeship abroad and a master's in Germany myself, I founded Fernweh Consulting to help Turkish students who want to study or intern in Europe but don't know where to start. I keep my fees deliberately low, because good guidance shouldn't depend on what you can afford.

Intercultural Adaptation Project

Enter Company, Netherlands · 2023

I led a team of exchange students working on cross-cultural collaboration at Enter Company, an organization that operates internationally. Together we designed and ran three workshops to build understanding and cooperation between colleagues from different cultural backgrounds.

Volunteer Teacher

BAMBU Volunteer Education Platform · 2022

I developed and taught engaging Turkish language and literature lessons for students in under-resourced communities.
